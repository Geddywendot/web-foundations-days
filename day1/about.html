About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple place to capture short ideas, reminders, and tasks so you can keep track of what matters. Use it to keep small tasks and thoughts easy to find throughout your day.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Type an idea in the New note field.
  3. Select Add Note to add it to your list creation.

Features

  • Quickly add notes from the Home page.
  • See your notes together in one list.
  • Use keyboard shortcuts to navigate and manage notes.

Keyboard shortcuts
